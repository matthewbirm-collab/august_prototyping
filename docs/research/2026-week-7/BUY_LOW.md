# Three conditional buy-low receivers entering Week 7

**2026 NFL regular season · Weeks 3–6 · half-PPR · research as of October 23, 2026**

My order is **Tee Higgins, Emeka Egbuka, Michael Wilson**. Higgins has the strongest demonstrated efficiency; Egbuka offers a target-volume recovery thesis but is out for Week 7; Wilson is the cheapest, most fragile bet on continued opportunity. These are trade inquiries with price limits, not three unconditional acquisitions. Actual offers and league transaction prices were not available, so a market discount has not been established.

## Window and method

This covers the last four **completed NFL weeks**, not each player's last four appearances. Week 7's Thursday game is excluded. Cincinnati's Week 6 bye leaves Higgins with three games; it is not a zero-point performance. The focus is wide receivers because routes, target depth and receiving efficiency directly address the requested analysis. This is a researched shortlist, not a validated league-wide ranking model.

The canonical inputs are Jerry Donabedian's RotoWire weekly usage tables: [Week 3](https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/nfl-box-score-breakdown-week-3-recap-usage-stats-137465), [Week 4](https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/nfl-box-score-breakdown-week-4-recap-usage-stats-139055), [Week 5](https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/nfl-box-score-breakdown-week-5-recap-usage-stats-140661), and [Week 6](https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/nfl-box-score-breakdown-week-6-recap-usage-stats-142315). The weekly Routes, Receiving, Tgts and AY columns are used; the trailing cumulative percentage columns are not substituted for weekly data.

- **YPRR:** total receiving yards / total routes. Measures production per opportunity to receive a pass.
- **aDOT:** total targeted air yards / total targets. Describes target depth, including incompletions; it is not average route depth.
- **TPRR:** total targets / total routes. Separates earning throws from simply being on the field.
- **Route participation:** total routes / total team dropbacks. The denominator includes sacks and scrambles.
- **Catch rate:** receptions / targets. Interpret alongside depth and quarterback play, not as a standalone receiver grade.
- **Half-PPR points:** 0.5 × receptions + 0.1 × receiving/rushing yards + 6 × receiving/rushing TDs. Figures are **before fumble deductions and league bonuses**; they are not certified league scoring totals.

All rates use summed numerators and denominators. Weekly ratios are never averaged to produce the four-week rate. [Weekly inputs](weekly-inputs.csv) and [unrounded aggregates](aggregates.json) accompany the report.

## 1. Tee Higgins: strongest efficiency, conditional discount

The buying argument is that a manager may overweight his latest 3.2-point outing and bye. The four-week production is substantially stronger. However, Week 4 supplies 58% of his yards, and Ja'Marr Chase exited that game. Removing Week 4 drops Higgins to **1.78 YPRR and 17.5% TPRR**. Do not project the spike game's workload forward. His adductor issue and reduced Week 5 role also matter. [Week 4 context](https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/nfl-box-score-breakdown-week-4-recap-usage-stats-139055), [Week 5 context](https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/nfl-box-score-breakdown-week-5-recap-usage-stats-140661).

The October 23 injury-status page lists no designation, but its practice table still says the final Friday status is due. Treat that as a verification limitation, not definitive clearance. [Status source](https://statchasers.com/nfl/players/tee-higgins/injury/).

**My price limit:** explore an exchange of a comparable WR2 whose recent scores have raised his perceived value. Do not pay a WR1 premium. Recheck the official injury report and workload before relying on an immediate start. If the owner values Higgins normally, the buy-low opportunity disappears.

## 2. Emeka Egbuka: opportunity recovery, for teams able to wait

His targets increased from 13 in Weeks 3–4 to 25 in Weeks 5–6. Yet the receiving efficiency remained weak. The attractive feature is sustained access to throws, not evidence that he is already producing efficiently. His rushing touchdown also makes his recent fantasy results look better than the receiving output alone. [Week 5](https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/nfl-box-score-breakdown-week-5-recap-usage-stats-140661), [Week 6](https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/nfl-box-score-breakdown-week-6-recap-usage-stats-142315).

**He is ruled out for Week 7 with concussion/foot injuries.** Tampa's official report lists Baker Mayfield as questionable, while subsequent reporting says he is expected to start. Availability must be checked again; the report does not assume a particular Egbuka return date. [Buccaneers injury report](https://www.buccaneers.com/news/buccaneers-panthers-injury-report-oct-23-week-7-2026), [Mayfield update](https://ca.sports.yahoo.com/news/baker-mayfield-set-to-start-for-bucs-week-7-game-vs-panthers-wr-emeka-egbuka-ruled-out-with-concussion-182028199.html).

**My price limit:** a bench/flex asset, with a meaningful injury discount. A team desperate for this week's points should pass. The thesis fails if his role or efficiency stays depressed after returning; quarterback improvement is a possibility, not a guaranteed remedy.

## 3. Michael Wilson: inexpensive volume, weakening trend

Two consecutive five-point games can create an opening, but the recent opportunity also deteriorated. Weeks 3–4: **30 targets on 89 routes**. Weeks 5–6: **14 on 72**. That is a TPRR decline from **33.7% to 19.4%**. Higher recent target depth has not offset fewer throws or weak conversion. Bourne received eight targets in each of the last two games, more than Wilson's seven. [Week 5](https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/nfl-box-score-breakdown-week-5-recap-usage-stats-140661), [Week 6](https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/nfl-box-score-breakdown-week-6-recap-usage-stats-142315).

**My price limit:** a spare bench player or modest flex swap. This is the lowest-confidence selection. Do not pay for the earlier double-digit-target role. Repeated sub-20% target rates or further route losses would weaken the case enough to stop pursuing him. His official player page provides roster confirmation, but this analysis does not establish final Week 7 clearance. [Cardinals profile](https://www.azcardinals.com/team/players-roster/michael-wilson/).

## Data reconciliation and limits

The initial [HeatRadar route dashboard](https://heatradar.app/nfl/routes), filtered to Weeks 3–6, showed Higgins with 110 routes, Egbuka with 147, and Wilson with 166; the weekly RotoWire inputs sum to 108, 138 and 161. Wilson's target total also differs: 43 versus 44. These discrepancies remain unresolved. All final metrics above consistently use RotoWire; they do not mix one provider's routes with another's yardage or aDOT. Future ingestion should reconcile providers before promoting these figures into the app.

There is no fitted expected-fantasy-points model here. Air yards minus receiving yards is **not** recoverable yardage: receiving yards include yards after catch, and some targets are uncatchable. Neither low catch rate nor a touchdown drought creates a debt that must be repaid.

Four weeks are too short to establish a stable talent estimate or calibrated forecast distribution. Opponents, quarterback changes, game scripts and injuries can dominate the sample. Descriptive standard deviations from three or four games would look precise while providing weak predictive information; none is presented as forecast uncertainty. The price limits are analyst judgment, not observed league prices or backtested rules.

For scale only, an assumed improvement of 0.25 YPRR over 35 routes adds 8.75 receiving yards, or 0.875 fantasy points before other changes. This arithmetic illustrates why modest efficiency improvement alone does not justify an expensive trade. It is not a player projection.
